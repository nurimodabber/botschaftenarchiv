THE UNIVERSAL HOUSE OF JUSTICE

1972-04-21

To the Bahá’ís of the World

Dearly loved Friends,

110.1 The opening of the final year of the Nine Year Plan sees the Bahá’í world community poised for overwhelming victory.  With grateful hearts we acknowledge the continuing confirmations which have attended its efforts and the Divine bounties which have never ceased to rain down upon this blessed, this ever-developing embryonic world order.

[signed: The Universal House of Justice]
