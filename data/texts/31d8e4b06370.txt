THE UNIVERSAL HOUSE OF JUSTICE

1967-06-25

To all National Spiritual Assemblies

Dear Bahá’í Friends,

44.1   12 November 1967, will mark the 150th anniversary of Bahá’u’lláh’s birth.  We call the entire Bahá’í world to joyful celebration, befitting an event so momentous to the fortunes of humanity.

[signed: The Universal House of Justice]
