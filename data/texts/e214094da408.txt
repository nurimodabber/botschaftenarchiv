THE UNIVERSAL HOUSE OF JUSTICE

19 March 2009

To all National Spiritual Assemblies

Dear Bahá’í Friends,

We are pleased to announce the appointment of Mr. Navid Serrano to the membership of the Continental Board of Counsellors in the Americas for the remainder of the present term, to fill the vacancy which resulted from the passing of Rebequa Murphy.

With loving Bahá’í greetings,

[signed: The Universal House of Justice]
