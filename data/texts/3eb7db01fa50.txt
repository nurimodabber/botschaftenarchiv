THE UNIVERSAL HOUSE OF JUSTICE

12 November 2018

To all National Spiritual Assemblies

With great joy we announce the appointment of the members of the Board of Directors for the Bahá’í International Development Organization, newly established at the Bahá’í World Centre, for the five-year term beginning 26 November 2018: Elisa Caney, Maame Brodwemaba Nketsiah, Lori McLaughlin Noguchi, Sina Rahmanian, and George Soraya.

[signed: The Universal House of Justice]
