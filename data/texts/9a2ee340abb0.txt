THE UNIVERSAL HOUSE OF JUSTICE

1983-11-07

To all National Spiritual Assemblies

Dear Bahá’í Friends,

381.1 Recent events in Iran following the banning of the Bahá’í administration indicate, unfortunately, the continuation of intense pressures against the defenseless Bahá’í community in that country.  Many of these pressures are being exerted by the authorities in the hope that the Bahá’ís will recant their faith and trade their love of Bahá’u’lláh for the comfort and security which the authorities offer to them in exchange.

[signed: The Universal House of Justice]
