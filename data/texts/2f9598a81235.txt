474378 Open Letter to Iranian Authorities from the National Spiritual Assembly of Iran

19 O CTOBER  1983

To all National Spiritual Assemblies Dear Bahá’í Friends, 378.1 You have already been informed of the disbanding of the National Spiritual Assembly and other Bahá’í institutions in Iran as a result of the new policy of the Iranian Government.  The National Spiritual Assembly, before disbanding itself, decided to issue an open letter to the authorities and the public of their country explaining the action of their Government and pointing out the unjust attitudes and accusations of the Government against the Bahá’í administration in that land.  Over two thousand of these letters were dispatched to important officials and other prominent people in Iran.

[signed: The Universal House of Justice]
