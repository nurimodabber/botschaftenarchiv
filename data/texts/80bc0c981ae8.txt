THE UNIVERSAL HOUSE OF JUSTICE

14 May 1975

To all National Spiritual Assemblies

Dear Bahá’í Friends,

The response to this request was encouraging, but it is obvious that there are many letters which have not yet been received.

2 If they are not in a position to provide such copies, they should kindly allow National Spiritual Assemblies to undertake this project on our behalf.

With loving Bahá’í greetings,

Department of the Secretariat

[signed: The Universal House of Justice]
