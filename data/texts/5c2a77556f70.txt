WITH TOTALLY FALSE ACCUSATIONS INVOLVEMENT POLITICAL ACTIVITIES.  IRANIAN

BAHÁ’ÍS SPECIALLY IN RURAL AREAS FACED WITH FRESH WAVE ARRESTS AND

TAKING REFUGE IN NEARBY LARGER TOWNS.  SUCH RUTHLESS ATTACKS REMAIN

UNCHECKED.  ATTEMPTS TERRORIZE BAHÁ’ÍS INTENSIFIED.  MORE INNOCENT LIVES AT STAKE....
