THE UNIVERSAL HOUSE OF JUSTICE

1992-04-05

it is with deep distress that we inform you that news has reached us to- day of the execution of mr. bahman Samandarí at evin prison in Tehran probably shortly before Naw-rúz. he was among the four friends arrested for meeting in his home about four years ago but who were released from several months of imprisonment following a global campaign in protest.

details concerning the execution are being sought. . . .
