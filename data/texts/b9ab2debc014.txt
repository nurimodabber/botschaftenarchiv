THE UNIVERSAL HOUSE OF JUSTICE

1983-07-04

To all National Spiritual Assemblies

368.1 ATROCITIES MOUNTING AGAINST BRETHREN CRADLE FAITH, NOW DIRECTED TOWARDS DEFENSELESS VILLAGERS NEAR SARI IN MÁZINDARÁN.  IN VILLAGE OF ÍVÁL OVER 130 BAHÁ’ÍS INCLUDING WOMEN AND CHILDREN WERE HELD CAPTIVE FOR THREE DAYS IN

WALLED-IN OPEN FIELD WITHOUT FOOD AND WATER.  WHEN PRESSURES TO RECANT

FAITH ACCEPT ISLAM FAILED THEY WERE ALLOWED TO RETURN TO THEIR HOMES.

HOWEVER SAME NIGHT, 1 JULY THEY WERE ATTACKED BY VILLAGERS AND FORCED

HIDE IN NEARBY FOREST.

368.2  FURTHER DISTRESSING NEWS TWO PROMINENT BAHÁ’ÍS TEHRAN, JAHÁNGÍR

HIDÁYATÍ AND AHMAD BASHÍRÍ, KIDNAPPED.  APPEALS TO AUTHORITIES SO FAR

UNHEEDED, ANY KNOWLEDGE THEIR ABDUCTION OR WHEREABOUTS DENIED....

UNIVERSAL HOUSE OF JUSTICE

369 Message to the European Youth Conference in Innsbruck

[signed: The Universal House of Justice]
