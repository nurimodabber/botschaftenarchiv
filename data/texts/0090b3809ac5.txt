THE UNIVERSAL HOUSE OF JUSTICE

1978-04-21

To the Friends gathered at National Bahá'í Convent

Beloved friends,

208.1  We joyfully hail the formation of seven more National Spiritual Assemblies, those of Burundi, Mauritania, the Bahamas, Oman, Qatar, the Mariana Islands and Cyprus; two in Africa, one in the Americas, two in Asia, one in the Pacific and one in Europe, raising to one hundred

[signed: The Universal House of Justice]
