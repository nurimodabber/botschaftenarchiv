296.1 RUTHLESS PERSECUTION DEFENSELESS COMMUNITY BAHÁ’ÍS IRAN FURTHER

INTENSIFIED THROUGH RECENT EXECUTION BY FIRING SQUADS OF SIX STAUNCH

MARTYRS, ONE IN TEHRAN ḤABÍBU’LLÁH ‘AZÍZÍ, FIVE IN DÁRYÚN NEAR ISFAHÁN,

BAHMAN ‘Á ṬIFÍ, ‘IZZAT ‘Á ṬIFÍ, ‘A ṬÁ’U’LLÁH RAW ḤÁNÍ, AHMAD RI ḌVÁNÍ, AND

399GUSHTÁSB THÁBIT-RÁSIKH.  LAST FIVE WERE IMPRISONED FOLLOWING LARGE-SCALE SIMULTANEOUS ATTACKS ON BAHÁ’ÍS IN THEIR HOMES AND ARREST OF SEVERAL OF

THEM IN VILLAGES NEAR ISFAHÁN.  FAMILIES MARTYRS WERE NOT INFORMED OF

EXECUTIONS WHILE RELATIVES THOSE EXECUTED IN DÁRYÚN ALSO NOT PERMITTED

CONDUCT BAHÁ’Í FUNERALS THEIR LOVED ONES, AND LAST THREE NAMED WERE

BURIED UNCEREMONIOUSLY IN MUSLIM CEMETERY.  NO ANNOUNCEMENT BY

AUTHORITIES WAS MADE ABOUT EXECUTION LAST FIVE....
