THE UNIVERSAL HOUSE OF JUSTICE

19 May 2019

To all National Spiritual Assemblies

We are delighted to announce the appointment of the members of the International Board of Trustees of Ḥuqúqu’lláh for a five-year term commencing on the anniversary of the Declaration of the Báb, 24 May 2019: Ho Yuet Mee, Enos Makhele, Manijeh Reyhani, Adam Robarts, and William Wieties. We are profoundly grateful to Marzia Rowhani Dalal for the exemplary service she has rendered to this institution.

[signed: The Universal House of Justice]
